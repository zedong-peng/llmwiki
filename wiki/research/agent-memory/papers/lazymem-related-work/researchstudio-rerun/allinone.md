## Summary

### Overview

The search covered three complementary formulations of budgeted long-term conversational-memory retrieval from 2024 through 2026. It returned 53 API records plus five independently verified additions. Because three high-signal connectors were rate-limited, the ranked API list overrepresents generic memory architecture, psychology, and low-specificity Crossref records.

### Trends

Of the 40 records with a known year, 17 are from 2024, 14 from 2025, and 9 from 2026; 13 lack a reliable year. The methodological progression is from compressive or structured memory and generic dense retrieval toward retrieval-stage control: sparse/dense hybrids, query-type routing, evidence-sufficiency loops, tiered raw escalation, and matched-budget evaluation. ACL-family and SIGIR papers contribute the strongest accepted work, while many 2026 items remain preprints.

### Key themes

1. **Write-time representation and compression:** summaries, event memories, graphs, and pre-storage reasoning trade retrieval convenience against fidelity (1, 4, 6).
2. **Conversational retrieval models:** dense, sparse, rewriting, and distillation methods adapt general IR to conversational state and session structure (19, 20, 34).
3. **Adaptive memory control:** cascades and route selection attempt to spend computation only when useful (11, 14, M1, M3).
4. **Evidence sufficiency and iterative repair:** multi-cycle retrieval judges whether accumulated evidence can answer the query and targets missing obligations (33, 40, M4).
5. **Raw evidence and lifecycle evaluation:** provenance, tier escalation, and budget accounting separate what was preserved from what the reader used (M5 and the previously verified parent corpus).

### Recommendations for reading

1. **Training-Free Lexical-Dense Fusion** establishes the strongest simple retrieval control and its cross-corpus boundary.
2. **AgentIR** is the direct prior art for a BM25-confidence semantic cascade.
3. **SelRoute** shows that cheap deterministic query-type routing already captures retrieval heterogeneity.
4. **EviMem** tests evidence-gap-driven iterative repair rather than score-only confidence.
5. **TierMem** provides the closest systems formulation of cheapest-sufficient evidence with authoritative raw fallback.
