Existing low-bit compression methods mainly follow two directions. Scalar or group-wise quantization is simple and compatible with efficient low-precision kernels, but its representation capacity becomes limited when the target budget approaches 2 bits per weight. Vector-quantized weight compression provides a richer block-level representation, but usually introduces explicit codebooks, index lookup, and additional storage accounting.
