Evaluate Efficient AI Inferencing with the Achronix VectorPath 815 Card

AI inferencing at scale is rapidly becoming unsustainable with GPU-based infrastructures. Designed for general-purpose workloads such as gaming, graphics, and AI training, GPUs are often over-provisioned and non-optimal for inferencing. The result is skyrocketing power consumption, mounting cooling costs, and a total cost of ownership (TCO) that spirals out of control.

Enter the Achronix VectorPath 815 PCIe card, powered by the Achronix 7t1500 FPGA — a purpose-built accelerator that redefines AI inferencing efficiency. Unlike GPUs, which rely on fixed-function cores and static compute pipelines, the VectorPath 815 card offers fine-grained, reconfigurable hardware tailored specifically to your model’s needs. It delivers custom data paths, native support for mixed-precision formats, and high-bandwidth GDDR6 memory for sustained throughput — all while consuming significantly less power.

In fact, the VectorPath 815 card provides at least a 3× TCO advantage over leading GPU solutions by maximizing compute per watt and enabling real-time reconfiguration as models evolve. This reconfigurability means you can adapt to tomorrow’s AI breakthroughs without replacing your hardware — just update the bitstream.

With Achronix, you are not just optimizing for today, you are building an inferencing platform that is cost-effective, adaptable, and future proof.

Achronix AI Console

The Achronix AI Console is your gateway to hands-on evaluation of high-performance AI inferencing, powered by the Achronix 7t1500 FPGA on the VectorPath 815 card. This web-based interface offers a ready-to-use playground featuring top open-source LLMs and advanced automatic speech recognition (ASR) models — all optimized for speed and efficiency.

With built-in how-to guides and seamless API key access, you can quickly explore the capabilities of Achronix AI solutions and accelerate your evaluation process.
